WoW Arm History

A study of a signal whose pooled expectancy is already known negative — run to find out whether it was ever positive, and when that stopped. Lane A: 104-week flag-matrix replay, 18 registered arms, read-only over already-graded records. This is not a hunt for a winner among the arms.

Spec docs/wow-arm-history-analysis-plan.md v0.1 Weeks 104 (2024-09-09 → 2026-08-31) Lane A arms 18, read-only NIFTY 50 fetch ^NSEI — reporting series only, never a gate Findings docs/wow_findings.md

What this study cannot conclude

104 weeks with a median of ~2 Control picks per week is a small sample carrying heavy market-factor correlation. It can distinguish “consistently negative” from “was positive, now negative”. It cannot establish a positive edge for any arm, and it cannot be the basis for graduating one. T4 is already killed by its own pre-registered DEV/VALIDATE protocol (2026-09-15 finding) — nothing here reopens that.

Q2 — which arm dominates paired vs. Control? Not a registered Test arm.

Mean paired weekly difference vs. control on weeks both have ≥1 pick, block-bootstrap CI. The paired difference is identical across net_pct / excess_eligible_pp / excess_bench_pp bases by construction — both arms share the same week's eligible-mean and benchmark, so those additive terms cancel in a same-week difference.

Paired delta vs. Control, rankedpp/week, CI95 whiskers, zero line

The biggest edge belongs to the untradeable baseline

The arm with the largest, CI-excludes-zero paired edge over Control is universe — buying the whole eligible universe equally every week: +0.918pp/wk, CI95 [+0.266, +1.773], not tradeable as a strategy, no risk cap, no stop, no sizing rule. That restates the regression guard's own fact: Control's filters underperform a coin flip from their own eligible universe by close to a point a week. t4_k7/8/9 show smaller, similarly CI-excludes-zero deltas (+0.67 to +0.71pp) — but T4 is already killed by its disciplined DEV/VALIDATE protocol; this pooled, undisciplined 104-week read does not reopen that. 17 arms were paired against Control; roughly 1-in-20 comparisons excludes zero by chance alone at an unadjusted 95% CI — 4 of 17 did, more than chance predicts but not overwhelmingly so, and none clear a Bonferroni-adjusted threshold. No arm graduates on this evidence.

Forward evidence is still one week — the dev-backtest doesn't change that

On the single graded live forward week, Control's 2 picks made +4.35% while T1's 14-name basket made -0.04% — a -4.40pp gap, but from n=1, which is exactly what the suppression rule exists to stop anyone from reading into. results/wow/paired_vs_control.csv's forward-mode row stays status="insufficient history (n=1, floor=13)" regardless of how much replay evidence accumulates above — the two are deliberately never pooled.

Lower than this plan's own earlier estimate — and that's the point

The plan's own §1.1 write-up quoted ~40% top-10 overlap and 12/13 flags persistent. This run's reproducible methodology (src/wow/diagnostics.py) comes back at 30.7% and 7/13 — lower on both counts. Treat this run's numbers as the ones to trust going forward; the earlier figures came from a less formal look. delta_flags_met (this week minus last week, same ticker) is now a panel column, making this testable as a timing signal later without another data build — not attempted in this pass.
